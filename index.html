Day 1 · Sat 7 Nov

The drive north. This is the whole day: about 124 km up, then the drive home after sunset. The oyster shed is open on Saturday and closed Monday and Tuesday, so this is the day for it.

1. Pie in the Sky

1296 Pacific Highway, Cowan. First stop, a pie shop above the old highway, about 46 km from the city.

2. Hawkesbury River Oyster Shed

6 Kowan Street, Mooney Mooney. About 6 km further. A meal by the river. The reel stops here because it is close.

3. Iris Lodge Alpacas

33 Dunks Lane, Jilliby. About 50 km on, inland. The animal stop. You leave the highway for this.

4. Long Jetty Wharf

Long Jetty, on the Central Coast. About 22 km from the alpacas. End the reel here by the water, then drive back to Sydney.
